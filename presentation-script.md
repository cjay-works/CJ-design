# CJ Design — Luxury Client Pitch Script

## How to use this script

This is a **10-part presentation script** for conversations with premium business owners, founders, property professionals, fitness studio owners, restaurateurs, creators, and luxury service brands. The tone should feel assured and consultative — never rushed, discount-led, or overly technical.

> **Core positioning:** You are not selling a collection of pages. You are creating the digital front door that makes a business feel credible, desirable, and easy to choose.

Replace the bracketed prompts with the prospect’s real business details. Use only verified testimonials, metrics, and case studies. Do not present the website’s “reaction we design for” copy as a client endorsement.

---

## Slide 1 — The opening

**On screen:** *Make your business impossible to ignore.*

**Say:**

“Before we talk about pages, features, or pricing, I want to start with the moment that matters most: the first impression.

When someone hears about your business, one of the first things they do is look you up. In those few seconds, your website answers a quiet question: *Does this business feel credible enough for me to trust?*

CJ Design builds modern websites that make that answer immediate — with clarity, polish, and a clear next step.”

**Pause and ask:**

“What do you want a new visitor to feel within the first ten seconds of discovering your business?”

---

## Slide 2 — The hidden cost of an unclear website

**On screen:** *Attention is expensive. Confusion costs more.*

**Say:**

“A business can have an excellent service and still lose opportunities online if the experience feels dated, difficult to understand, or hard to act on.

The visitor may not say, ‘This website has poor information architecture.’ They simply leave, delay the enquiry, or choose the business that feels easier to trust.

Our role is to remove that hesitation. We turn what you do into a digital experience that feels as considered as the business itself.”

**Discovery prompt:**

“Where do you feel the current experience loses momentum — first impression, explaining your offer, showing proof, or getting people to enquire?”

---

## Slide 3 — The CJ Design approach

**On screen:** *Understand. Shape. Build. Launch.*

**Say:**

“We work in four focused stages.

First, we understand what makes your business different and what the next customer needs to feel. Then we shape your story, services, and offer into a clear digital direction. We build the experience responsively, so it feels right on the device your customer actually uses. Finally, we launch with the essential foundations covered — domain, hosting, mobile responsiveness, basic SEO, and clear contact paths.

This is not about adding more screens. It is about making the right decision feel easier.”

## Slide 4 — How the website helps the business grow

**On screen:** *A website should pull its weight.*

**Say:**

“A stronger website helps the business in five practical ways:

1. It creates a stronger first impression when someone discovers you.
2. It keeps the business discoverable 24/7, even when you are closed or busy.
3. It builds trust through clear information, proof, structure, and polish.
4. It creates more qualified enquiries because people understand what you offer.
5. It makes action effortless through forms, WhatsApp-ready CTAs, booking paths, menus, or checkout flows where relevant.

The result is not just a more beautiful website. It is a more confident business presence.”

**Personalise with:**

“For [CLIENT BUSINESS], that could mean [specific outcome: more property enquiries / more trial memberships / more table reservations / more qualified product conversations].”

---

## Slide 5 — Choose the right digital experience

**On screen:** *The right structure for the business you are building.*

**Say:**

“We design around the customer journey your business actually needs.

For a real estate brand, that means property listings, detail pages, enquiry forms, WhatsApp CTAs, and a path from browsing to conversation. For a gym or fitness studio, it means services, classes, trainers, gallery, and membership enquiry. For a restaurant, it means menu, gallery, reservation or enquiry options, location, and contact.

For e-commerce or a portfolio, the priority is showing the work or products beautifully, then making the next action — purchase, enquiry, or conversation — obvious.”

**Important positioning:**

“We do not offer SaaS products. We build focused websites around the business in front of us.”

## Slide 10 — Objection handling

### “I only need something simple.”

“Absolutely. Simple does not have to mean generic. We can keep the scope focused while making sure the experience still communicates trust, explains the offer clearly, and gives people an easy next step.”

### “I already have Instagram.”

“Instagram is excellent for attention and ongoing content. Your website is the place you control — the place that explains the full offer, answers questions, supports search discovery, and gives a prospect a clear path to enquire.”

### “Why not use a template?”

“A template can provide a starting structure. Our value is in shaping the structure around your customer, your positioning, and the perception you want to create. The result should feel like your business, not a theme shared by hundreds of others.”

### “Can you guarantee more sales?”

“No responsible studio should promise a specific sales result from design alone. What we can do is improve the conditions around growth: stronger trust, clearer messaging, easier discovery, and less friction between interest and action.”

### “The price is higher than a basic website.”

“That is fair. We are not positioning this as the cheapest way to put pages online. We are building a digital front door that represents the value of your business every day. The right comparison is not only the build cost — it is what a clearer, more credible experience can help you communicate and convert over time.”
